<p>Due to the unprecedented times of the pandemic, the <a href="https://students.iitk.ac.in/snt/">Science and Technology Council, IIT Kanpur</a> decided to conduct all Summer'20 Projects in an online mode. Although it was a new paradigm for everyone, all members worked hard to make it a big success.</p>
